Get in touch!

At Vision Studios, we wanted to create more than just a place to rehearse or record. We wanted a space where artists feel comfortable, welcome, and free to create. Located in Atlanta, our studio is a home for musicians and creatives to work on their craft, collaborate, and bring their ideas to life. Beyond our rehearsal and recording spaces, Vision Studios also features an indoor live-performance venue designed for artists to share their work with a real audience. From intimate performances and showcases to live events and special productions, our venue gives artists a place to step out of the studio and onto the stage. Whether you’re developing your next project, preparing for a performance, recording new material, or putting on a show, Vision Studios is here to help bring your vision to life. Come create with us, book your space, and let’s make your next project something worth experiencing.
